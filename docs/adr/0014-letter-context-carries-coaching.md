# The Letter context carries Letter coaching, not the Job analysis's candid sections

> Amends ADR 0011: the Letter context takes the Job analysis's Strengths, Letter coaching and CV under-sells, and no longer its Weaknesses, Screener risks and Gaps to close.

The Job analysis has two readers with opposite needs. The candidate needs it candid: whether to apply, and what a screener will hold against them. The Applicant Agent and the Seasoned Recruiter need a coach, because by the time they run the candidate has decided to apply, and the letter's only job is to get the interview. So job-analyzer now writes a **Letter coaching** section for the agents: the strengths to amplify, and each weakness framed positively inside the Evidence bank's facts, with "mention / don't mention". The Letter context carries that section in place of the candid ones, and both agents read the same Letter context. The rest of the Job analysis stays as candid as before.

Before, the candid sections went straight to the agents, and the letters repeated them. One job-analyzer run advised "say plainly in the cover letter that payments would be a new domain". Reviewed letters opened sentences with "Internal customers were never our main focus…" and "Fintech would be a new domain for me…", telling the reader the candidate was not a full fit before saying what they had done.

## Considered Options

- **Rewording Weaknesses and Gaps to close in a coaching tone.** Rejected: those sections are written for the candidate, who needs the straight answer, and one section can't serve both readers.
- **Keeping the candid sections in the Letter context for the Recruiter only,** so it can judge how gaps are handled. Rejected: the Recruiter writes the Letter plan and the replacement text, so the candid framing would come back through it. It judges gap handling against Letter coaching instead.

## Consequences

- A cover-letter Run on an Application whose Job analysis has no Letter coaching writes it first, from the Job analysis and the Evidence bank, as it already rewrites a missing Evidence bank. The Job analysis's score and Verdict don't change.
- Letter coaching frames only facts the Evidence bank holds. A positive framing that needs a fact or number the bank doesn't hold is an unsupported claim, as it would be anywhere in the letter.
